Part 4: AI Solution Design for a Business Problem

Task 8: Final Solution Summary
Problem: Doctors were spending too much time in reviewing the medical images like x-rays, CT scan, and other radiology scans with the risks involved due to the delays.
Proposed AI solution: It is recommended to use CNN-based image classification to triage urgent cases and reduce the report review time eliminating manual intervention.
Required data: X-ray/scan images with disease labels.
Expected business impact: With the solutioning, it gives faster diagnosis, reduced workload, and better patient care.
Risks & mitigation: Since is it a model based Solution, there can be issues with misdiagnosis which needs a diverse training data, secure storage, and mandatory doctor review.
